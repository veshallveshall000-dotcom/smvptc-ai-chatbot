About the College:  
SMVPTC is a diploma engineering institution established with the aim to provide technical education to the region. Approved by AICTE and affiliated to the relevant state technical board. Known for its strong placement focus and quality infrastructure.  

Courses Offered (Diploma – 3 Years Typical):  
- Diploma in Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)  
- Diploma in Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)  
- Diploma in Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EEE)  
- Diploma in Mechanical Engineering (Mech)  

Fee Structure (Approximate – for Academic Year 2025):  
- Diploma in Computer Science & Engineering: Total ~ ₹80,000 (approx breakdown ₹25,000 + ₹25,000 + ₹30,000)  
- Diploma in Mechanical Engineering: Total ~ ₹75,000  
- Diploma in Electrical & Electronics Engineering: Total ~ ₹70,000  
Additional costs: Application fee ₹500; Exam fee ₹1,000; Library/Technology fees ₹2,000; Miscellaneous ₹3,000.  

Placement & Training:  
- Training & Placement Cell provides soft-skills, communication, group discussion, mock interviews, technical skill workshops.  
- For the academic year 2024: Highest package ~ ₹6.7 LPA; Average ~ ₹4.2 LPA; Median ~ ₹3.9 LPA. Number of offers ≈ 50; Students placed ≈45; Companies visited ~20.  

Infrastructure & Facilities:  
- Laboratories (CNC machines, advanced electrical/electronics labs) and workshops.  
- Digital library, hostel & transport facilities.  
- Hostel & mess fees (for other courses) for illustration: Single sharing ~₹20,000 hostel + ₹15,000 mess = ~₹35,000 per annum.  

Vision & Motto:  
- Vision: To become a world-class centre in providing globally relevant higher education in the field of engineering and technology with human values.  
- Motto: “Our motto is to produce well trained technocrafts as per the industrial needs.”
